About me

Hello - I am Chris, and I am completely dog-mad.

I am a London man, born and raised here, married, and living with four dogs of my own. That is not a marketing line - it is the reason this business exists. Once you have four sets of paws in the house, you learn fast that dogs do not need grand gestures. They need routine, a proper walk, and someone who actually notices when they are not quite themselves.

So that is what I do. I walk dogs across West London the way I walk my own - the long way round, in small groups, at the pace that suits the dog rather than the clock. Nervous rescues, elderly dogs who prefer a gentle wander, and young ones with far too much energy are all very welcome; they just get different walks.

If I am late, you will hear from me before you notice. If your dog has a bad day, you will hear about that too.
